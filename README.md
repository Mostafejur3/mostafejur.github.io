### LinkedIn Analytics Dashboard

**Purpose:**  
The goal of this project was to analyze engagement efficiency on LinkedIn to understand why interaction plateaued despite growth in followers. I examined content timing, format, and audience demographics to identify patterns that influence engagement performance.

**Tech Stack:**
* **Excel:** Cleaned and structured post-level performance data for impressions, engagements, and followers.
* **SQL:** Queried and aggregated engagement metrics to calculate month-over-month growth.
* **Tableau:** Built an interactive dashboard visualizing KPIs, audience demographics, and engagement trends.
